<p>This privacy policy explains in plain language which personal data {game_name} collects, what it is used for, how long it is kept and who can see it.</p>

<h2>Who runs this website</h2>
<p>This website is run by its operator. The operator's name and postal address are listed on the <a href="{contacts_url}">contact page</a>.<!--mail--> You can reach us by email at {contact_mail}.<!--/mail--></p>

<h2>Visiting the website</h2>
<p>Each time you open a page, the web server automatically records:</p>
<ul>
<li>your IP address,</li>
<li>the date and time of the request,</li>
<li>the requested address and the referring page,</li>
<li>the browser and operating system (user agent),</li>
<li>the status code and the amount of data transferred.</li>
</ul>
<p>We use this data to deliver the website and to keep it stable and secure, for example to detect and fend off attacks. Server log files are deleted after 7 days at the latest, unless they are needed as evidence of a specific security incident. In that case they are kept until the incident has been resolved.</p>

<h2>Account and player data</h2>
<p>To play, you need an account. When you register and play, we collect:</p>
<ul>
<li>account name, email address and password (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text),</li>
<li>language, time zone and display settings,</li>
<li>registration time, IP address at registration, time and IP address of the last login, and failed login attempts,</li>
<li>one player per universe: player name and game data such as planets, buildings, research, fleets, resources, points, achievements, combat and espionage reports.</li>
</ul>
<p>We need this data to provide the game you signed up for. Without an email address and a password, no account can be created.</p>
<p>The nature of the game makes some data visible to other players: player name, coordinates of your planets, alliance, points and rankings (statistics, hall of fame), and the content you choose to publish (alliance texts<!--board-->, board posts<!--/board-->).</p>

<h2>Communication within the game</h2>
<p>When you use the communication features, we store their content together with the sender, the recipients and the time: private and alliance messages, buddy requests, notes, support tickets<!--board-->, and posts, comments and votes on the board<!--/board-->. This is done so that the features work. Moderators can read support tickets and, if a breach of the rules is suspected, the reported content.<!--messages--> Messages are deleted automatically after {message_days} days.<!--/messages--></p>

<h2>Fair play and protection against abuse</h2>
<p>To keep the game fair and to enforce the rules (for example the ban on multi-accounting and account pushing), we use:</p>
<ul>
<li>IP addresses of active sessions and logins, compared between accounts to detect several accounts played from the same connection,</li>
<li>session records (session ID, IP address, last activity), which are deleted after the session expires,</li>
<li>notes by the game team on individual accounts, and records of bans with their reason and duration,</li>
<li>if there is a concrete suspicion of a breach of the rules for a single account, a record of the pages requested by that account with the time of each request. This record is deleted once the investigation is complete.</li>
</ul>
<p>When you register, we check the domain of your email address against a public list of disposable email providers. The list is downloaded to our server, and your address is not sent anywhere. Measures such as bans are decided by members of the game team, not by an automated process.</p>

<h2>Emails</h2>
<p>We send emails to the address you registered with when they are needed to run your account: activation of your account, resetting your password, confirming a change of your email address, warnings before an inactive account is deleted, and important notices about the game (for example the start of a new universe). We do not send newsletters or advertising.</p>

<h2>Bot protection</h2>
<p>Registration and the lost-password form are protected against automated abuse by a small computation that your browser solves on its own when you open the form. It is checked on our own server; no third-party service is contacted, and no cookies or tracking identifiers are used for it.</p>

<h2>Cookies and local storage</h2>
<p>We only use cookies and browser storage that are needed to provide the service you request:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>2Moons</strong>: session cookie that keeps you logged in. It expires 12 hours after your last activity, or when you log out.</li>
<li><strong>v0_device</strong>: random device ID (2 years). Only a hash of it is stored per account, for 90 days after the last visit, to detect several accounts played from the same browser.</li>
<li><strong>lang</strong>: remembers the language you selected.</li>
<li><strong>account</strong>: remembers that this browser has been used to log in, so the start page can show the login first. It contains no personal data.</li>
<li>Local storage: display preferences of the game interface (for example the last opened message category). They stay in your browser and are not sent to us.</li>
</ul>
<p>We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, and we do not create user profiles for marketing.</p>

<h2>Who can see your data</h2>
<p>Your data is handled by members of the game team (administrators and moderators) as far as their tasks require it. Technical service providers work on our behalf and only on our instructions: the hosting provider of the servers and, where applicable, the email delivery service. Other players see the data described under "Account and player data" and "Communication within the game". We pass data on to authorities only if we are legally obliged to. We do not sell personal data.</p>
<p>Links to external services (for example Discord or the wiki) only take you to those services. Data is handled there only once you open the link, under the privacy policy of the respective provider.</p>

<h2>How long data is kept</h2>
<ul>
<li>You can delete your account in the account settings. It is removed after a waiting period of {deletion_days} days, during which you can cancel the deletion.</li>
<!--inactive--><li>Players who have not been active for {inactive_days} days are deleted automatically. Where the game sends warning emails, you receive one before the deletion.</li>
<!--/inactive--><li>Accounts without any player are deleted after 30 days without activity.</li>
<li>Server log files: no longer than 7 days, see "Visiting the website".</li>
<li>Data we must keep for legal reasons, or need to handle legal claims, is kept only as long as necessary.</li>
<li>Database backups contain the data at the time of the backup. They are kept for a limited time and replaced in rotation.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Your choices</h2>
<p>You can change your account data in the account settings and delete your account there at any time. If you would like to know which data we hold about you, want it corrected or removed, or have any other question about your data, contact us using the details on the <a href="{contacts_url}">contact page</a><!--mail--> or by email at {contact_mail}<!--/mail-->. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local data protection law and may be able to contact your local data protection authority.</p>

<h2>Security</h2>
<p>All connections to this website are encrypted (TLS), passwords are stored only as hashes, and access to personal data is limited to the members of the game team who need it. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.</p>

<h2>Changes</h2>
<p>We update this privacy policy when the website or the legal requirements change. The version published on this page applies.</p>
